# Summary: R7 | Model: mistral

The meeting was a detailed design meeting for a remote control product, specifically a banana-shaped remote control. The meeting was led by a project manager and attended by several team members. The agenda included presentation of the prototype, evaluation criteria, finance, production evaluation, and closing.

The prototype presented was a banana-shaped remote control with a base station for charging and locating the remote. It had two scroll wheels for channel selection, volume control, and tele-text browsing, a turbo button for quick channel navigation, and a teletext button for accessing teletext services. The remote was powered by solar cells and rechargeable batteries.

The team members evaluated the remote control based on several criteria such as fanciness, handiness, functionality, cool features, ease of use, and whether they would buy it or change their current remote control for it. The scores varied, but overall, the team members seemed to like the remote control.

The finance evaluation showed that the production costs were under the target of 12.50 euros. The team discussed potential cost savings and ideas for future improvements, such as simplifying the circuit board and expanding the remote control's capabilities to control other devices in the room.

The meeting ended with the team expressing satisfaction with the design and expressing enthusiasm for testing a real prototype at home.
