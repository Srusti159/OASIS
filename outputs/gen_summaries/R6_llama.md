**Meeting Summary**

**Project Overview**

The meeting was a kick-off meeting for a project to design a new remote control. The project manager, Laura, introduced herself and the other team members, including David (industrial designer), Andrew (marketing manager), and Greg (user interface designer). The objective of the project is to design a remote control that is original, trendy, and user-friendly.

**Project Method**

The project will follow a three-stage design process:

1. Functional design: individual work and meeting
2. Conceptual design: individual work and meeting
3. Detailed design: individual work and meeting

**Discussion**

The team discussed the project finance, including the selling price of €25 and the production cost of €1250. They also discussed the design requirements, including the need for a unique selling point and the importance of considering international markets. The team brainstormed ideas for the remote control, including the possibility of adding new features such as lighting control and note-taking capabilities.

**Action Items**

The team agreed on the following action items:

* David (industrial designer) will work on the actual working design of the remote control
* Greg (user interface designer) will work on the technical functions of the remote control
* Andrew (marketing executive) will think about the requirements of the remote control
* The team will receive instructions via email

**Next Steps**

The next meeting is scheduled for 30 minutes, and the team will continue working on the project. The functional design stage is the next stage of the project.

**Key Takeaways**

* The project requires a unique selling point to differentiate it from existing remote controls
* The team needs to consider international markets and design requirements
* The remote control should have features that go beyond traditional television control, such as lighting control and note-taking capabilities
* The production cost is a key factor in determining the design requirements of the remote control.
