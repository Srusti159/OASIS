# Summary: R6 | Model: mistral

The meeting was a kick-off for a project to design a new remote control. The project will be divided into three stages: functional design, conceptual design, and detailed design. Each stage will involve individual work and meetings. The remote control is expected to be original, trendy, and user-friendly.

The participants introduced themselves as Laura (project manager), David (industrial designer), Andrew (marketing manager), Greg (user interface designer), and Craig. They shared their favorite animals and their characteristics as an icebreaker.

The discussion then moved to the project finance. The remote control will be sold for €25, and the goal is to make €50 million. The production cost is capped at €1250. There was a debate about whether the international market would influence the design, considering potential differences in regions, frequencies, and symbols.

The meeting concluded with individual tasks for the next 30 minutes. David is responsible for the working design in the functional design phase, Greg for the technical functions design, and Andrew for the user requirements specification. Specific instructions will be sent to them by their personal coaches. The next meeting is scheduled for 30 minutes later.
