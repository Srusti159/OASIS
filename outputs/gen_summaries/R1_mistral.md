# Summary: R1 | Model: mistral

The meeting was about the detailed design of a product, with the agenda including a presentation of the product, evaluation, finance discussion, and closing. The product, named Real Reaction, is an electronic device that combines fashion and electronics, with features such as a locator, cradle, scroll wheel, and a new zapping scroll function. The production cost is estimated at 15.8 euros per unit.

Pedro and Kevin, the User Interface Designers, presented the product, demonstrating its features and design. The product was well-received, with the marketing team finding it marketable due to its unique features, reliability, comfort, ergonomics, and environmentally-friendly aspects.

The team discussed the possibility of a lifetime guarantee for the product, which was considered a good idea. They also discussed the potential for the product to be upgradable, similar to a SIM card in a phone, but this idea was met with some reservations due to potential complications.

The finance discussion revealed that the initial budget was exceeded, but the costs are now within the revised budget of under 20 euros to build. The project was then evaluated, and the team agreed that it was a success, particularly in terms of room for creativity, teamwork, and the new ideas found.

The meeting concluded with a celebration, as free coke was provided at the cafeteria. The team was congratulated for their work, and it was agreed to close the meeting and return to their paperwork.
