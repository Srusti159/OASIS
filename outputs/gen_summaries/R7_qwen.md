### Comprehensive Summary of the Meeting

#### Agenda Overview
The meeting focused on the detailed design of a new remote control, specifically a "Mando Banana." The agenda included reviewing the minutes of the previous meeting, presenting the prototype, discussing evaluation criteria, evaluating the financial aspects, and making final decisions on the product's feasibility.

#### Detailed Design Presentation
- **Prototype Presentation**: 
  - The remote control was designed to resemble a banana, complete with a banana-shaped base station that serves as a charging dock and antenna. The remote itself was ergonomically designed with two scroll wheels for channel and volume control, a turbo button, and a teletext button.
  - The remote could be activated by pressing the turbo button, and the teletext button allowed navigation through teletext pages using the scroll wheels.
  - The base station featured leaves that acted as antennas, enhancing signal reception.
  - Power sources included solar cells and rechargeable batteries, with the base station connected to a power outlet for charging.

- **Design Materials and Features**:
  - The remote was made of elastic plastic, colored yellow to match the banana theme.
  - The base station included a button for calling the remote and was designed to be lightweight, similar to a banana.
  - The remote was equipped with a regular chip for control, and the cost was a critical factor in the design.

#### Evaluation Criteria
- **Criteria**:
  - The device was evaluated based on its fancy appearance, handy design, functionality, cool features, ease of use, and whether it would be worth buying for 25 euros.
  - Users rated the device on a scale from 0 to 7, with 7 indicating strong agreement.

- **User Feedback**:
  - **Fancy**: Users generally agreed that the device was fancy, with ratings ranging from 6 to 7.
  - **Handy**: The device was considered handy, with ratings from 6 to 7.
  - **Functional**: Ratings varied, with some users giving it a 4, while others gave it a 6.
  - **Cool Features**: The locator feature and scroll buttons were considered cool, with ratings from 5 to 7.
  - **Ease of Use**: The device was deemed easy to use, with most users giving it a 7.
  - **Price**: Opinions were mixed, with some users rating it as 2 or 5, while others gave it a 6.
  - **Would Change Remote**: Users were asked if they would change their existing remote for the Mando Banana, with ratings ranging from 2 to 3.

#### Financial Evaluation
- **Cost Calculation**:
  - An Excel sheet was prepared to calculate the production costs, aiming to keep the cost under 12.50 euros.
  - Components included batteries, electronics, and the banana-shaped remote.
  - The team aimed to reduce costs by using plastic instead of rubber and simplifying the circuit board.

- **Budget Analysis**:
  - The total cost was estimated to be around 11 euros, with an additional 90 cents for the base station.
  - The team decided to invest the extra profit in research and development.

#### Project Evaluation
- **Process Evaluation**:
  - The team discussed the project process, noting that they had used a whiteboard and digital pens for brainstorming.
  - Suggestions were made to conduct a focus group with the target audience to gather more feedback.

- **New Ideas**:
  - The team proposed expanding the remote's functionality to control other devices, such as DVD players and mood lighting, in future versions.

#### Conclusion
- The team concluded that the Mando Banana was a viable product, with a feasible design and cost structure.
- They recommended producing a real prototype for further evaluation and testing.
- The meeting ended on a positive note, with the team expressing satisfaction with the progress made.

### Key Takeaways
- The Mando Banana remote control was well-received in terms of its design and features.
- Cost optimization was crucial, and the team successfully kept the production cost under the target of 12.50 euros.
- Future iterations should focus on expanding the remote's functionality and conducting user testing to refine the product.
