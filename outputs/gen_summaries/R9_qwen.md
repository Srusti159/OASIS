# Summary: R9 | Model: qwen

### Comprehensive Summary of the Meeting

#### Overview
The meeting focused on the conceptual design of a remote control, aiming to reach a decision on its design and functionality. The meeting included presentations from various team members and discussions on key aspects such as appearance, ease of use, and technical specifications.

#### Agenda
1. **Opening**
2. **Goal**: Reach a decision on the concept
3. **Presentations**: Three fast presentations
4. **Decision on Remote Control Concepts**
5. **Closing**

#### Presentations and Discussions

1. **Market Research and Fashion Trends (Fabien, Project Manager)**
   - **Key Findings**:
     - **Fancy Look and Feel**: Highest priority (1st).
     - **Technological Innovation**: Second priority (2nd), but less important than fancy look and feel (half as important).
     - **Ease of Use**: Third priority (3rd), less important than technological innovation (half as important).
   - **Fashion Trends**:
     - Fruit and vegetable motifs will be prominent in clothing, shoes, and furniture.
     - Material feel will be spongy.

2. **User Interface Design (Hamed Ketabdar, User Interface Designer)**
   - **Design Principles**:
     - Place most frequently used buttons in the middle and make them larger.
     - Ensure the remote control is comfortable to hold, with rounded edges.
     - Minimize energy consumption.
   - **Personal Preferences**:
     - Cover less frequently used buttons.
     - Place volume and channel change buttons on the sides.
     - Consider using a speech recognizer, though it consumes more energy.

3. **Technical Specifications (Engineering Team)**
   - **Screen and Size**:
     - 7-inch TFT screen (initially thought to be 7 cm x 7 cm, but can be cut to fit).
     - Board size: 7 cm x 7 cm (can be adjusted to 5 cm x 10 cm).
   - **Energy Source**:
     - Batteries (AA cells) and solar cells.
     - Solar cells are necessary for speech recognition and LCD.
   - **Case and Shape**:
     - Banana-shaped design suggested due to the company’s yellow color.
     - Consider using a touch screen for ease of use.
   - **Speech Recognition**:
     - Feasible but requires careful placement to ensure adequate sunlight for solar cells.

#### Decisions and Actions
- **Remote Control Concept**:
  - **Fancy Look and Feel**: Highest priority.
  - **Technological Innovation**: Second priority.
  - **Ease of Use**: Third priority.
  - **Material Feel**: Spongy.
  - **Shape**: Banana-shaped with a touch screen.
  - **Energy Source**: Batteries and solar cells.
- **Next Steps**:
  - **Look and Feel Design**: Industrial Manager.
  - **User Interface Design**: User Interface Manager.
  - **Product Evaluation**: Marketing Expert.
  - **Prototype Development**: Hamed and Peter to work together on the first prototype.

#### Conclusion
The meeting concluded with a clear direction for the remote control design, emphasizing the importance of a fancy look and feel, technological innovation, and ease of use. The team agreed on the banana-shaped design and the use of a touch screen. The next meeting is scheduled in 30 minutes, with specific tasks assigned to each team member.
