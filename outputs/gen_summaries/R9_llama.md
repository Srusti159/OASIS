# Summary: R9 | Model: llama

**Meeting Summary: Conceptual Design of Remote Control**

**Date:** Not specified

**Attendees:**

* Fabien (Project Manager)
* Hamed (User Interface Designer)
* Bob (Marketing Expert)
* Peter (Industrial Manager)
* Ahmed (Speaker 00)
* Other speakers (Speakers 01, 02, 03, 04)

**Meeting Objective:**

The objective of the meeting was to reach a decision on the concept of the remote control. The goal was to finalize the design and features of the remote control based on the input from the team members.

**Agenda:**

1. Introduction and goal of the meeting
2. Presentations from team members
3. Discussion and decision-making
4. Next steps and individual actions

**Presentations:**

1. **Speaker 00 (Ahmed):** Presented recent results from remote control market research and fashion trends. The key findings were:
	* Fancy look and feel is the highest priority
	* Technological innovation is the second priority
	* Easy-to-use is the third priority
	* Fruit and vegetable motif will be the most important theme in clothes, shoes, and furniture
	* The feel of material this year is expected to be spongy
2. **Speaker 02 (Hamed):** Presented ideas for the user interface design, including:
	* Easy-to-use feature
	* Large buttons in the middle of the remote control
	* Rounded shape corners for comfort
	* Not very energy-consuming
	* Personal preferences:
		+ Less frequently used buttons should be hidden by a cover
		+ Some buttons, like volume change, can be placed on the side of the remote
		+ Usage of a speech recognizer
3. **Speaker 03 (Bob):** Presented ideas for the technical functions design, including:
	* 7-inch TFT screen
	* Solar cells for energy
	* Infrared unit for communication
	* Main board with all the circuits
	* Interface to the microphone and graphical card for the TFT

**Discussion and Decision-Making:**

The team discussed and debated the ideas presented by each speaker. The key decisions made were:

1. **Shape and size:** The team agreed on a shape and size for the remote control, with a preference for a smaller size (5x10 cm) and a rounded shape.
2. **Case:** The team decided to use a banana as the shape of the remote control, with a spongy layer on the outside and a plastic cover that can be peeled off to reveal the buttons.
3. **User interface:** The team decided to use a touch screen interface, with a smaller LCD screen.
4. **Speech recognition:** The team decided to use a speech recognition system, with a solar cell to provide energy.
5. **Next steps:** The team assigned individual actions to each member, including:
	* Industrial manager (Peter) to work on the look and feel design
	* User interface manager (Hamed) to work on the user interface device
	* Marketing expert (Bob) to do the first product evaluation
	* The team to work together to create a prototype

**Conclusion:**

The meeting concluded with a clear direction for the design and features of the remote control. The team is now working on creating a prototype and finalizing the details of the design. The next meeting will be held in 30 minutes to review progress and discuss further.
