#### Detailed Design Meeting
- **Presenter:** Krista Pavlov, Project Manager
- **Topic:** Detailed Design of a Remote Control
- **Key Points:**
  - The remote control was designed to incorporate elements of fashion and technology, with a focus on strawberries as the primary design element.
  - The remote control features include power ON/OFF, volume control, LCD controls, speech recognition, and location finding.
  - The material used is a spongy titanium alloy, chosen for its modern and practical qualities.
  - The design is intended to be simple and perfect, with customizable colors and shapes.

#### Prototype Presentation

#### Financial Evaluation
- **Budget:** The total cost of the prototype was calculated to be €17.80, which exceeded the budget of €12.50.

- **Final Cost:** After adjustments, the cost was reduced to €12.30, staying within the budget.
