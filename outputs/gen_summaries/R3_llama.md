# Summary: R3 | Model: llama

**Meeting Summary**

**Meeting Topic:** Concept Design for Remote Control

**Attendees:** Industrial Designer (ID), User Interface Designer (UID), Marketing Expert (ME), and other team members.

**Objective:** To finalize the concept design for the remote control, incorporating the latest trends and user preferences.

**Summary of Discussions:**

1. **Previous Decisions:** The team reviewed the previous meeting's decisions, including the simplicity of the design, the use of a wheel for channel control, and the protection of channel buttons with a plastic cover.
2. **Industrial Design Presentation:** The industrial designer presented the components design, highlighting the standard chip for remote control, case study for wheel sensor, and standard BP. The team discussed the use of a wheel sensor, which was deemed too expensive, and considered alternatives.
3. **User Interface Design Presentation:** The user interface designer presented the interface concept, emphasizing the simplicity and ergonomics of the design. The team discussed the placement of buttons, the use of a wheel, and the importance of symmetry for left-hand users.
4. **Market Trends:** The marketing expert presented the latest trends in the remote control market, highlighting the importance of a fancy look-and-feel, technological innovation, and ease of use. The team discussed the potential use of a fruit or vegetable shape for the remote control, with a spongy material and a color scheme that matches the company's brand.
5. **Decisions:** The team made the following decisions:
	* Use a standard battery and a cheap on-print chip.
	* Design the case to resemble a banana or a pear, with a spongy material.
	* Use a wheel on the top and buttons on the side, with a heart-shaped design and a spongy cover.
	* Ensure symmetry for left-hand users.
6. **Next Steps:** The team agreed to work on the final look-and-feel design, user interface design, and product evaluation. The industrial designer and user interface designer will work together to create a prototype, which will be evaluated for user requirements.

**Action Items:**

* Industrial Designer: Finalize the look-and-feel design according to the team's decisions.
* User Interface Designer: Finalize the user interface design and create a prototype with the industrial designer.
* Marketing Expert: Evaluate the product and ensure it meets user requirements.
* Team: Review and finalize the design before the next meeting.

**Next Meeting:** The next meeting will be held in 30 minutes, and team members will receive further instructions via email.
